These files are the LATIN SUBSET only, taken from the Google Fonts CDN and served
from this origin. The full family also carries Devanagari, which is roughly four
times the bytes and nothing on this site sets type in it.

They are self-hosted on purpose. The Content-Security-Policy is `font-src 'self'
data:`, so a request to fonts.gstatic.com would be blocked, and the app is built
to run with no internet at all.

  poppins-400.woff2   Regular    body
  poppins-500.woff2   Medium     the tracked brand line
  poppins-600.woff2   SemiBold   prices, spec values
  poppins-800.woff2   ExtraBold  the ghosted model name

31 KB in total for all four.
