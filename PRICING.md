# Pricing MotoKE

How to price it, and — more importantly — how to defend the price in the room.

---

## Start from what one car is worth to them

Used-car gross margin runs **10–15% of the sale price**. On a typical Nairobi yard:

| | |
|---|---|
| Average car | ~KES 2,500,000 |
| Gross margin at 12% | **~KES 300,000 per car** |
| A yard selling 10–20 cars a month | KES 3M–6M gross a month |

**That KES 300,000 is the only number that matters in the negotiation.** Everything you
charge should be quoted as a fraction of one car.

If MotoKE helps them close **one extra car a quarter**, it has produced KES 100,000 a
month in gross profit. Price well under that and the argument is over before it starts.

---

## What I would charge

### Setup — KES 100,000, one time

Do not give this away. It buys:

- Their stock loaded, priced and photographed
- Their branding, branches and opening hours
- Their lender panel configured with real published rates
- Staff trained on the console
- The domain pointed and live

That is a week of real work. It also **filters out the tyre-kickers** — a dealer who won't
pay a setup fee won't use the product either, and a free pilot that dies quietly costs you
more than the fee was worth.

*Frame it as:* **one third of the margin on one car.**

### Monthly — tiered by stock

| Tier | Stock | Monthly | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | up to 30 cars | **KES 15,000** | Small yard, one branch |
| **Standard** | up to 100 cars | **KES 25,000** | The one most will take |
| **Group** | unlimited, multi-branch | **KES 40,000** | Several branches, more staff |

*Frame it as:* **KES 25,000 is 8% of the margin on one car.** They need to sell one extra
car a *year* to break even. Anything beyond that is profit.

### Annual — 10 months for 12

Offer **KES 250,000/year** instead of 25,000×12. It gets you paid up front, kills monthly
chasing, and Kenyan businesses are used to annual software billing. Roughly half will take
it if you offer it properly.

---

## What to charge extra for

Keep these separate. They are real costs and bundling them means eating them.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Photography** | KES 2,000–3,000 per car, or their own photos free. This is the biggest hidden cost — don't absorb it. |
| **Extra branches** | KES 5,000/month each beyond the tier |
| **Data migration** | KES 20,000 if they have stock in a spreadsheet or another system |
| **Custom domain and email** | At cost plus a small handling fee |
| **Training beyond the first session** | KES 10,000 per half day |

---

## Your own costs, so you know your floor

| | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Hosting (Cloudflare Containers or Fly.io) | ~KES 700–2,500 |
| Domain | ~KES 150 |
| **Real cost per dealership** | **under KES 3,000** |

At KES 25,000 the margin is roughly 88%. **Support time is your actual cost**, not
infrastructure — which is why the price has to be high enough to pay for answering the
phone. This is the single most common mistake: pricing at 5,000, winning ten dealers, and
discovering you have a full-time support job that earns 50,000 a month.

---

## The first three dealerships

Discount them, but do it in writing and for something in return.

> **Founding partner: KES 12,000/month for the first year, then standard pricing.**
> In exchange: a written testimonial, a reference call with other dealers, and honest
> feedback when something is wrong.

Two things this gets right. It is **explicitly a launch price with an end date**, so you
are not stuck at 12,000 forever. And it makes them a partner rather than a bargain hunter,
which changes how they treat you when something breaks.

**Do not go below 10,000.** Software nobody pays for is software nobody uses, and a
dealership that paid nothing will not open the console on a Monday morning.

---

## Two models worth considering later

### Per funded deal

**KES 3,000–5,000 per application that actually gets funded.** Perfect alignment: you earn
when they earn. Dealers understand commission.

The catch is verification — you are relying on them reporting, or on the tracker being the
system of record. Once every deal genuinely runs through MotoKE you can count them
yourself, and this becomes the strongest model you have. **Offer it in year two, not now.**

### Free storefront, paid finance engine

Give away the catalogue; charge for the finance comparison, the tracker and the console.
Gets you in the door and the paid half is the half they cannot live without once they have
used it.

Risky as an opener — free products get treated as free — but a good fallback for a dealer
who is genuinely hesitant.

## Answering the price objection

**"That's expensive."**
> "It's 8% of the margin on one car. If it helps you close one extra car a year, it's
> paid for. How many did you lose to a decline last month?"

**"Can we try it free for a month?"**
> "The setup is a week of my work, so that part is chargeable. But the monthly fee starts
> after you've had it live for thirty days — if it isn't earning by then, we stop and you
> keep the site."

That is a much better offer than a free trial: they are committed, you are paid for the
work, and the risk you carry is the recurring fee — which is the part you should be
confident about.

**"Can you build it once and we own it?"**
> Quote **KES 800,000–1,200,000** for a one-off build with source, no support and no
> updates. Most will not take it, and the number makes the KES 25,000 look sensible.
> If they do take it, you have been paid properly for three years of subscription up front.

**"The other guy charges 5,000."**
> "He's selling you a website. This decides which lender approves your customer, tells
> you which cars are costing you money, and lets your customer track their application.
> Ask his product what a buyer on 150,000 a month can afford."

---

## What I would actually do

1. **KES 100,000 setup + KES 25,000/month** as the standard price. Print it. Do not
   negotiate the list price — negotiate the launch discount instead.
2. **First three dealers at 12,000/month for year one**, in writing, for testimonials.
3. **Photography quoted separately**, always.
4. **Push annual at 250,000** — it solves your cash flow while you are small.
5. **Revisit per-funded-deal pricing in year two**, once the tracker is where deals live.

The pricing is not the hard part. **Two reference dealerships who will take a phone call
from a third** are worth more than any pricing page, which is why the founding-partner
discount buys testimonials rather than just goodwill.
